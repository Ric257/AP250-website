About Me

I'm a blacker who started uploading Black MIDI content since July 2019, and although I don't upload videos so frequently on the internet, I'm still alive. Currently, I'm 19 years old, I born in May 2007. I'm from Lima Metropolitan, Peru, and yes, I can speak Spanish too, but for the commodity of my most readers, I write this in English.

How did I get into Black MIDI? That was 8 years ago (2018) where I saw a video from Carlos S.M. That's where I knew about Black MIDI's existence, but I wasn't interested in uploading something until 2019 (And also, at the time my content was completely different, you know where to search for those videos). Although I had mental breakdowns during all the time as a blacker, I always appreciated the genre as it is.

To finalise, this website is still in WIP, this is a primitive work, and I will introduce new features, more content and maybe, some fixes to the UI. Be patient for it, I'm still new in the CSS, HTML and the programming topics.
